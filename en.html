About me

I hold a Master's degree and am a Ph.D. candidate in Linguistic Studies at the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG), and I have a degree in Letters — Portuguese/English from the Federal University of Viçosa (UFV). My path is marked by a combination of love for languages and fascination with digital technologies, which allows me to work at the intersection of language teaching, academic research and technology consulting.

I conduct research in Applied Linguistics, with a special focus on the use of games and digital tools as allies of the learning process. As a teacher and researcher, I constantly seek to build bridges between academic theory and innovative pedagogical practices, turning the classroom into a dynamic and engaging environment.

"Video games made me learn English in a fun and immersive way. Technology has always attracted me, and the moment I started my Letters degree, I felt I had found my place in the world."

Teaching

I worked as a professor in the Department of Languages at the Federal University of Viçosa, where I taught courses in English Language, Digital Technologies, and Language Teaching and Learning. My classroom experience ranges from basic and advanced instruction to intensive and extensive extension courses, always focusing on student engagement.

Beyond direct teaching, I have a strong presence in pedagogical coordination. I am a member of the coordinating committee of the English Language Extension Course (CELIN) at UFV and served as a Mentor Professor, guiding new teachers so they can effectively integrate innovative technologies and methodologies into their teaching practices.

Publications

My scientific output focuses on the field of Applied Linguistics, exploring how digital technologies can enhance additional language teaching. Throughout my career, I have investigated in depth the affordances of digital games and Ludic Language Pedagogy, analyzing how play can promote deeper and more enjoyable learning.

Currently, my research focus is on the impact of Generative Artificial Intelligence on language teaching. I investigate how these tools can expand teacher agency and support the redesign of pedagogical practices, seeking solutions that combine cutting-edge technology and educational intentionality to prepare students for a digital world.

Projects

Besides my work in teaching, I have solid experience in several areas of computing. For about 20 years, I have used Linux and open-source software, as an enthusiast and advocate of free software as an essential path toward building a fairer, more transparent and more accessible digital society.

I developed the UFV Map in Minecraft, reproducing the main buildings of the Federal University of Viçosa campus inside the game, at a scale close to reality. I also collaborated on the development of PhotoLoco, a community micro-game created by teachers from the Ludic Language Pedagogy community, reinforcing the commitment to creating playful and collaborative learning environments.

In the field of educational systems development, I am the creator of planit, an LMS (Learning Management System) with live presentation features to energize digital teaching. I am currently the lead developer of SabIA (Saberes sobre Inteligência Artificial para a Aprendizagem de Línguas — Knowledge about Artificial Intelligence for Language Learning), a platform curating AI tools for language teaching, developed by the DELTA group at UFMG.

Consulting

I am open to educational partnerships and consulting for language schools, regular schools and test-prep courses. My goal is to help institutions modernize their teaching methods through the strategic integration of new technologies and active methodologies.

I offer training and courses focused on the use of digital games and Artificial Intelligence tools in language teaching. If you want to turn your institution's language education into a more technological, playful and efficient experience, let's talk about how I can help your team reach these goals.
